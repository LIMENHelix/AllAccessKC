Refund Policy

Last updated: May 17, 2026
This Refund Policy is the legally binding English version. Machine-translated versions of other pages on this site are provided for reader convenience only; this English text governs.

1. Overview

All Access KC sells a single product: a $5 digital Kansas City visitor guide. This Refund Policy explains when and how you can get your money back, and how to ask.

2. 14-day refund window

You can request a full refund within 14 days of your purchase date, no questions asked, for any reason. To request a refund, email info@allaccesskc.com with:

  • Your Stripe receipt or purchase confirmation
  • The email address used at checkout, if available
  • (Optional) The reason for the refund — we read every one and use them to improve the guide

We process refund requests within 1–2 business days. Stripe then takes 5–10 business days to return the funds to your original payment method.

3. Technical-issue refunds (no time limit)

If the guide will not unlock on your device, will not load, or otherwise does not work for you, email us at any time and we will either fix the problem or refund you. This applies even after the 14-day window has closed.

4. When refunds are not available

The 14-day window is generous on purpose. After it expires, refunds are at our discretion. We will generally decline refund requests when:

  • More than 14 days have passed and the guide has worked correctly
  • You have used the guide substantively (made reservations through links, navigated multiple sections) and then asked for a refund without citing a problem
  • Your access has been revoked for violation of the Terms of Service (for example, attempting to share or resell access)

5. Partial refunds

We do not offer partial refunds. Each purchase is either fully refunded or not refunded.

6. Chargebacks

If you initiate a chargeback with your bank or card issuer rather than emailing us, we will share the relevant purchase record with Stripe to respond to the chargeback. We would much rather just refund you directly — please email us first.

7. Contact

Refund requests and questions: info@allaccesskc.com.
