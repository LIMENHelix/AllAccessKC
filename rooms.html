How it works

Eight rooms, one city, no forms

Every room below is a live, open board. You don't make an account — when you say something, you're handed a friendly Kansas-City-flavored nickname (think Plaza Pigeon or Burnt-End Bandit) and you're in. Scroll to a room and it wakes up automatically.

🤝

House rules. These rooms are public and lightly moderated. Be kind and be respectful — it's a public board where anyone can read along. No doxxing and please don't share sensitive info (your address, phone, payment details, anything you'd regret a stranger seeing). Treat newcomers like guests in your hometown, because that's exactly what they are.
