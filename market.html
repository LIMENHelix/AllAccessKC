Read this before you meet anyone

Buy and sell safe

This is a free, open board. We don't process payments, hold money, or vet posts — so a little street smarts goes a long way. The vast majority of KC neighbors are exactly who they say they are. Cover these basics and keep it that way.

  • Meet in safe public places. Daytime, busy spots. Many KC police stations have a marked "safe exchange" lot built for exactly this — use it.
  • Never wire money or share bank details. No Zelle to strangers, no gift cards, no "deposit to hold it." Pay in person, in cash or a trusted app, when you have the item in hand.
  • Inspect before you pay. Plug it in, sit on it, test it, look it over. If it's not what the post said, walk away — no hard feelings.
  • Bring a friend for big items. Couches, appliances, anything you're hauling — a second person makes pickups safer and easier.

We don't process payments or vet posts. All Access KC is a free bulletin board — use common sense, and trust your gut if something feels off.

How to write a listing that moves

Be specific

Brand, size, color, condition. "Solid oak table, seats 6" beats "table for sale."

Name the area

Cross-streets or neighborhood — Waldo, Northland, Westport. People want to know how far.

Price it clearly

A number, "OBO," or "Free." Vague prices get scrolled past. Free stuff goes fast.

Say when

Pickup windows and your preferred contact save everyone a dozen messages.

Heads up: it's text listings only right now — no image upload yet — so paint the picture in words.
