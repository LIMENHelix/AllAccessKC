Match-day logistics

Getting to Arrowhead

The smartest arrival isn't driving. Here's how locals do a big day at the complex.

Parking

Prepaid only — no cash

Parking at the complex is prepaid in advance; gates do not take cash on match day. Buy your spot ahead of time or you'll be turned around. Expect higher demand and prices than a normal game.

Better than driving

Streetcar + rideshare

The free KC Streetcar and rideshare beat fighting traffic to the complex. Stage downtown, ride in, and skip the parking scramble and the post-match exodus entirely.

Security

Clear-bag policy

Arrowhead enforces a clear-bag policy. Bring only what's allowed in a clear bag, arrive early, and build in extra time for screening on these high-traffic match days.
